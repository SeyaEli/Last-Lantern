LAST LANTERN — MODES EDITION VALIDATION
25 October 2026

87 integration checks passed against the final embedded game script.
The harness uses Node and native Canvas with a minimal DOM and preloaded
copies of the same embedded assets. It exercises game behavior directly.

Checked:
- New accounts, legacy migration, and level-200 save validation.
- All ten camp tracks stop at 200 and do not charge beyond the cap.
- All eighteen class skills can be learned, equipped and trained to 200.
- Campaign traverses all 600 waves and exactly 30 boss encounters.
- Boss waves do not advance until the boss is defeated.
- Story traverses six acts and thirty discoveries, then saves its ending.
- Endless continues beyond total wave 600 and increases enemy vitality.
- Enemy health, damage, group size and population limits grow as intended.
- Continue restores health, position, level, weapon, cooldown bonuses,
  pending blessings, boss breaks and projectile hit history.
- Invalid expedition data is discarded while valid account progress remains.
- Resuming a defeated boss's break does not duplicate its rewards.
- Saving during a wave-100 story discovery resumes into the next map;
  it cannot accidentally create wave 101 in a campaign/story map.
- All eighteen skills produce their expected effects after cast delays.
- Enemy shots wait for visible casting windup and dead enemies do not fire.
- Enemy walk, windup, attack and death atlas frames are distinct.
- Dash afterimages follow actual travelled points, freeze on pause and expire.
- No external scripts, images, fonts, fetch calls, sockets or runtime asset
  downloads are required by the standalone HTML.
- Management routes produce their expected content in the DOM harness.
- Level-200 bonuses and long-run damage, movement, armor and haste stay bounded.
- All three optional objective types grant their rewards only once.
- Normal hero damage waits for the action contact frame.
- Every ranged weapon emits at its registered rendered tip in both directions.
- All nineteen action sheets decode to 151 nonempty, distinct poses.
- All 31 weapons retain complete armed hero art and animated locomotion.
- A 150-enemy, up-to-320-projectile simulation updates with bounded objects.

Visual verification:
Current hero action atlases, the class roster, six realm views, and enemy
movement/casting/death sequences were rendered from the actual game drawing
functions and inspected. The supplied and imagegen-normalized sheets are
included in art/provided-actions/. The art notes describe which animation
sequences use painted poses and which use procedural rigs.

Verification limits:
The local browser preview was blocked when origin permission was declined.
No subsequent workaround browser connection was used. The final menu layout,
responsive behavior and actual browser frame rate therefore still need a
live browser playtest. This edition's checks are engine integration tests
and offline Canvas art renders, not a claim of 60 FPS on every device.

A prolonged combined native rendering stress run did not complete in the
test harness. An isolated six-frame native render at 150 enemies and 320
projectiles completed. The normal 90-frame load check measures simulation
updates only. Extended browser play and memory profiling remain unverified.

Full-session difficulty, gold earned per hour, and the grind required for
legendary/mythic purchases have not been measured with human playtests.
The price and health curves are implemented tuning choices, not validated
retention or commercial-success claims.

Story maps now use solid tile geometry. Campaign and Endless retain their earlier arena scenery.
Thirty boss encounters use variants of existing enemy and ruler art,
rather than thirty separate hand-painted boss animation sheets.

Nine-weapon update: all nine reference weapons have eight-frame attack and
skill bindings. Weapon tips are registered per frame. Skill release waits
for frame four, and faster normal attacks accelerate their pose sequence
while completing recovery before restarting. Three additional checks pass.
Original sheets, packed atlases and prompts are in art/weapon-actions-v2/
and art/NINE-WEAPON-ANIMATION-NOTES.txt. Source-cell flare edges are tapered
at draw time; no extra background or character is painted over the hero.
