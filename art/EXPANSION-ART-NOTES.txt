LAST LANTERN — EXPANSION AND ANIMATION ART NOTES
25 October 2026

Generation method: built-in imagegen, using the user's supplied references. No runtime art downloads.
Six detailed realm backgrounds were generated from the provided gothic cemetery and lava-map references. New realm rulers and elite cutouts were generated with transparency, then packed as local sprites.
Thirty boss encounters use those sprites and existing enemy silhouettes with code-native rigs and attack-pattern variants, rather than thirty separately painted sheets.

Ten supplied attack/skill sheets were normalized through imagegen to preserve the painted hand/weapon grips, separate overlapping poses and complete clipped portions. Their 79 poses were then cropped, ground-registered, packed into transparent atlases and embedded in the HTML. Some wide glow effects are trimmed at pose boundaries to keep neighboring characters out of the atlas. Actual hero bodies and held weapons are retained.
Walking, fallback attacks, dashing, enemy wings, limbs, spirit tails, windups, recoil, hurt and death use Canvas pose rigs. Hero dash trails sample actual historical positions. Projectiles release at registered weapon tips. Other class weapons retain their complete armed hero art with procedural motion.

Supplied source and normalized PNGs, packed atlases and registration metadata are in provided-actions/. Maps are in realm-maps/. New rulers are in realm-bosses/. Earlier generation notes remain in ART-NOTES.txt and CLASS-ART-NOTES.txt.
