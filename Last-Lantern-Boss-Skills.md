# Last Lantern: boss skills

Two named skills for every boss, across Campaign, Story and Endless. Press **B**, or use **Skills** on the boss health bar, to read the active boss’s attacks.

Warnings lock before release. Bosses attack more often deeper into the journey. Below half health they chain two skills; below quarter health they chain three. Every cast keeps its full warning and recovery gap. Stuns interrupt chains and trigger 3.6 seconds of stagger protection; burst damage can still break healing rituals. In Story Mode, solid cover blocks beams, charges and projectiles. Boss death cancels that boss’s active fields and projectiles.

- **Lantern Shards** — 0.95s warning; 5.8s normal cooldown. Fires a spread of fast shards in the marked direction. The aim locks before release.

### Wave 60: Rootbound Oracle

- **Root Constellation** — 1.25s warning; 7.5s normal cooldown. Marks several impact sites near your position. They stop tracking before they erupt.
