LAST LANTERN — THE DEBT OF DAWN
Playable story adventure · 28 October 2026

START
Open Last-Lantern.html, choose New Game, then Play Story. Select your hero,
weapon and class skill before starting. Refresh an already-open game tab.
Existing gold, heroes, equipment, skills and camp upgrades remain available.
Continue upgrades an older Story expedition to the new explorable maps.

THE FIRST ROAD
Read the arrival conversation, then walk toward Mara beside the camp lantern.
Move close and press E, or use the on-screen Interact button. Speak to Mara
to open the first warding gate. Follow the open corridor to Finch, accept
his objective, and defeat the district's waves and guardian. Return to him
afterward to open the next gate. People and quest objects are marked on the
minimap. M opens the explored map; Q opens the quest log.

WHAT CHANGED
Story now has six explorable chapters, each with a safe entrance and five
connected encounter districts. Each district has 20 waves; its guardian
appears at 20, 40, 60, 80 or 100. Ordinary waves advance after every enemy is
defeated. Waiting does not clear a wave. Gates close during combat.

Witnesses need more than a guardian defeated. Rescue the courier, recover
three physical memory items, operate both mechanisms, and choose whether
to carry people or proof first. Complete the conversation before leaving
a district. After the fifth district, interact with the glowing exit to
travel to the next chapter. Your levels and build carry forward.

The story follows the company's search for the names consumed by Brann's
lantern: the woods, furnace, observatory, citadel, drowned abbey, and first
flame. Thirty NPC objectives reveal the cost of the beacons. Choices on the
road affect bonuses, later dialogue, the journal and the final epilogue.
The final choice offers two endings.

SOLID MAPS
Story maps use real tile geometry. Walls and pillars stop walking, dashing,
enemies and projectiles. Enemy navigation follows the same terrain. Crates
block shots and movement, but can be broken by shooting or interacting.
Floor and wall textures decorate the same geometry used for collision.
These are handcrafted room layouts, not randomly generated dungeons.
Campaign and Endless retain their existing arena maps and wave rules.

PREPARATION AND SAVES
Rest by the entrance lantern or speak with Nox. His single field dressing
per chapter costs 120 x chapter gold and restores 40% health. Story choices
can grant protection and healing, or a damage bonus. Recruitment and
equipment prices retain the earlier progression rules.

P pauses. Save & return keeps your position, current room, enemies, damaged
crates, collected memories, mechanisms, dialogue page and decisions.
Continue resumes that expedition. Completed objectives are not rewarded
again by loading a save. Defeat offers the saved checkpoint and build.

CONTROLS
WASD / arrows / drag: move. Attacks aim automatically at visible enemies.
Space: equipped skill. Shift: dash. E / Interact: talk, collect, operate.
M: explored map. Q: quest log. P: pause.

VERIFICATION
87 engine integration checks pass using the embedded game script and native
Canvas. Six-map connectivity, NPC reachability, all 600 story waves, gates,
collision, projectile cover, save/restore and both choice paths were checked.
Previews are native Canvas renders. Live browser layout, mobile play and
full human difficulty/playtime testing have not been performed.

BOSS ABILITIES
All 30 bosses have two named skills. Warning circles, charge lanes, landing
sigils and open wedges appear before release. Use cover against beams and
projectiles, step out of marked impact circles, and dodge charges sideways.
Stuns interrupt a cast; enough burst damage can break a healing ritual.
Press B or use Skills on the boss health bar for a paused description of
the current boss. The full list is in Last-Lantern-Boss-Skills.md.
