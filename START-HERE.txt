LAST LANTERN — THE DEBT OF DAWN
Offline modes edition • 25 October 2026

START PLAYING
Double-click Last-Lantern.html. Everything required to play is embedded in
that file. No installation, account, server, or internet connection is needed.
If the game is already open, refresh the tab to load this edition.

HOME AND YOUR SAVE
Continue restores your saved expedition, including its hero, weapon, skills,
wave, health, blessings, cooldowns, enemies, and projectiles.
New Game opens mode selection. Starting a new expedition keeps your gold,
purchased heroes and weapons, trained skills, journal, and camp upgrades.
If another expedition is saved, you must confirm before replacing it.

Use Pause > Save & return to home whenever you want to stop or manage camp.
The expedition also saves periodically, on rewards, and when leaving the tab.
Use Export save for a portable backup, including the current expedition.
Use Import save at camp to restore an exported file. Older valid saves keep
their gold and unlocks. Saves are local to the browser and file location;
export before moving the file or switching browsers.

GAME MODES
CAMPAIGN — THE SIX BEACONS
Six scrolling 2400 x 1500 maps, each with 100 waves. Bosses arrive at waves
20, 40, 60, 80, and 100. Boss waves stop advancing until the boss is defeated.
That is 600 campaign waves and 30 named boss encounters, with different
enemy silhouettes and escalating attack patterns across the five sections.
Campaign allows starting at an already-unlocked map.

STORY — THE DEBT OF DAWN
Brann's last lantern consumes memories to keep the darkness away. Discover
who built the six beacons and choose what a sunrise is worth. Six acts,
thirty boss discoveries, an introduction to each act, two ending choices,
and a persistent journal accompany the same 100-wave map structure.
Rescue survivors, defend beacons, and recover relics for optional rewards.
No character purchase is required to understand or finish the story.
Story expeditions begin in the Whispering Woods.

ENDLESS — THE LONG NIGHT
Begin in the woods and survive indefinitely. Bosses appear every 20 waves;
the map changes every 100. After the sixth map, another stronger cycle begins.
Best wave and score are stored locally. Endless does not grant campaign wins.

THE SIX REALMS
1. Whispering Woods — The Hollow King
2. Cinder Quarry — Cindermaw
3. Frostfall Ruins — Eclipse Matriarch
4. Ashen Citadel — The Iron Tyrant
5. Drowned Necropolis — The Grave Cantor
6. Obsidian Throne — The Ashen Sovereign
Each final ruler is preceded by four named wardens. Boss variants reuse
the game's enemy art with distinct rigs and progressively stronger patterns.
There are not thirty separately painted boss sprite sheets.

WAVES AND CHECKPOINTS
Normal waves last about 18–24 seconds, becoming quicker in later sections.
Boss fights extend this time. New enemies gain health every wave and a
larger health increase on later maps. Spawn groups and population caps grow.
Bosses telegraph their projectiles, charges, and marked ground attacks.

Defeating a boss banks rewards, heals 25%, and refreshes skill and dash.
Continue from its break screen to create the next section's checkpoint.
Defeat offers a retry with the build saved at that checkpoint. Earned gold
stays banked. Cleared checkpoint rewards are not granted again on Continue.
Entering the next campaign/story map restores 35% health and carries levels
and blessings forward. Level damage growth is capped at 6x starting damage;
attack rate, movement speed, armor, and regeneration also have safety caps.

CONTROLS
WASD / arrow keys: move. Space: equipped class skill. Shift: dash.
P / Escape: pause. Attacks automatically aim at targets within class range.
Touch: drag the battlefield to move; use the Dash and skill buttons.
Walk, attack, skill, hurt, and dash poses animate complete armed heroes.
Dash afterimages record where your hero actually travelled.
Enemy animations include movement, wings or spirit tails, windup, attack,
charge, hurt, and defeat poses. Avoid the ring or line before impact.

freezes, delayed impacts, healing fields, guards, spirit shots and sentries.

CAMP — TEN TRACKS, 200 LEVELS EACH
Tempered steel: starting weapon damage
Quickdraw: attack speed, with a minimum interval
Keeper's heart: starting health
Brighter flame: skill strength
Trailblazer: movement speed, with diminishing returns

Watchtower: up to 30% permanent damage reduction

Every track shows current benefit, next benefit, level, price and milestone.
Purchases apply to new expeditions. Continuing a saved expedition preserves
the equipment and build with which it was saved.

MAP GUIDE
The camera follows the hero across a world larger than the visible arena.
The minimap shows enemies, limited healing shrines, caches and objectives.
Pale squares mark objective locations. Stand close to rescue survivors;
hold the marked beacon/relic circles to make progress on other objectives.
Map buildings and environmental art are decorative, not collision obstacles.
